# Speaking — micro plan

Fits [weekly-plan.md](../weekly-plan.md). Default block: **15 minutes**. Always record.

## Each speak-micro

1. Timer 45–90 seconds
2. Answer in English only
3. Listen once: mark **one** repair (grammar, pause, or vague word)
4. Record the same answer again (shorter is fine)

## Week shape

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Tue | Part 1: work / weekday / study |
| Thu | Part 1 or easy Part 2 (1 minute prep + 1 minute talk) |
| Sat | Same Part 2 again, then one Part 3 *why* question |

## By phase

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| A–B | Part 1 complete sentences; time markers (*usually, these days*) |
| C | Part 2 four bullets; one concrete story |
| D–E | Part 3: compare, speculate, *it depends* |

## Bank of Part 1 starters (rotate)

- How do you spend a normal weekday?
- What do you do to relax after work?
- Do you prefer studying in the morning or at night?
- Tell me about a skill you want to improve.
