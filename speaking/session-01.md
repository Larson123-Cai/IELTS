# Session 01 — Everyday Part 1

Goal: three short spoken answers (about 20–40 seconds each). Use a phrase from `vocab.md` when one exists.

These topics overlap with Writing’s first drill (study / part-time work), so new words can move between chats.

## Questions
1. Do you work or are you a student?
2. What do you like most about your studies or your work?
3. Do you think it is a good idea for students to have a part-time job? Why?

## How to answer
- Start with a direct answer, then add one reason and one example.
- Speak in full sentences. Do not list words.
- Target length: 3–5 sentences per question.

## After you answer
The tutor will:
1. Keep at most 3 corrections
2. Give improved sentences you can reuse
3. Log new phrases in `vocab.md` and patterns in `errors.md`
4. Offer 3 expressions for the next round
