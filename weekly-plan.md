## Constraints

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Work | Full-time |
| Daily time | 60–120 minutes |
| Session size | 15–25 minutes (stack 3–5 blocks) |
| Horizon | 3–5 years |
| Start | ~band 5 |
| Target | band 7.5 |

**Rule:** a finished 15-minute block beats a skipped 90-minute block.

---

## Daily LEGO blocks

Pick 3–5. Stop when you hit 60–120 minutes.

| Block | Minutes | What you produce |
|---|---|---|
| L-commute | 15–20 | 3 shadowed sentences, or 1 short section replay |
| Vocab | 10–15 | 5 cards reviewed; 1 original sentence in `vocab.md` |
| Read-micro | 20 | 1 paragraph: main idea + 3 unknown phrases |
| Speak-micro | 15 | 1 recorded answer (45–90 seconds) |
| Write-micro | 20–25 | 4–6 sentences, or 1 PEEL paragraph |
| Error-loop | 15 | 3 old errors rewritten from `errors.md` |
| Mock-mini | 40–50 | 1 Listening section **or** 1 Reading passage (weekend) |

**Busy-day floor (about 50 minutes):** L-commute + Vocab + one output block (Speak **or** Write).

**Energy-low floor (about 25 minutes):** Vocab + 10 minutes of shadowing. Do not do a full mock on these days.

## Default week (about 8–11 hours)

Weekdays stay under 90 minutes. Saturday is the only longer block. Sunday is lighter.

| Day | Block 1 | Block 2 | Block 3 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | L-commute 15 | Vocab 15 | Write-micro 25 | 55–80 |
| Tue | L-commute 15 | Read-micro 20 | Speak-micro 15 | 50–75 |
| Wed | L-commute 15 | Vocab 15 | Write-micro 25 | 55–80 |
| Thu | L-commute 15 | Read-micro 20 | Speak-micro 15 | 50–75 |
| Fri | L-commute 15 | Error-loop 15 | Write rewrite 25 | 55–80 |
| Sat | Mock-mini 45 | Speak 20 | Vocab 15 | 80–100 |
| Sun | Light L 15 | Review `errors.md` + next week 15 | Optional read 20 | 30–50 |

Weekly skill mix (normal week):

| Skill | Time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | ~2.0 h | commute + Saturday |
| Reading | ~1.5 h | Tue / Thu / optional Sun |
| Writing | ~2.0 h | Mon / Wed / Fri rewrite |
| Speaking | ~1.0–1.3 h | Tue / Thu / Sat |
| Vocab + errors | ~1.0–1.5 h | daily glue |

If a week is brutal at work: keep **Wed write + Fri rewrite + daily 15-min listen**. That week still counts.

## 12-week cycle (repeat for 3–5 years)

Do not invent a new system every month. Repeat this cycle. Only the **materials and difficulty** go up.

| Weeks | Mode | Main output |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Input + control | Accurate sentences; 1 paragraph/week; Part 1 answers |
| 5–8 | Output + exam shape | 1 short Task 2 / week; Part 2 recordings; 1 reading passage |
| 9–11 | Mixed exam | Mini-mocks; rewrite old work; timed pieces |
| 12 | Recover + measure | No new books. Review `vocab.md` / `errors.md`. One diagnostic. |

**Each year ≈ 4 cycles.** After cycle 12-week review, raise difficulty one notch (see phases below).

---

## 3–5 year phases

Hours assume 1–1.5 hours most days, not a perfect 2 hours.

### Phase A · Months 1–3 · Make it automatic (~band 5.0)

- Build the weekday blocks until missing a day feels strange
- Core grammar: tense, articles, S-V agreement, because/so/but
- 8–12 new usable chunks per week, not 50 abandoned words
- **Exit:** 5 days/week for 8 weeks; can write 120 words in 25 minutes with meaning clear

### Phase B · Year 1 remainder · Foundation (~5.0 → 6.0)

- Listening: Section 1–2 first; numbers, spelling, signpost words
- Reading: paragraph main idea before full tests
- Writing: one idea per paragraph; concrete examples
- Speaking: Part 1 fluent; Part 2 with a 4-bullet card
- **Exit:** unofficial ~6.0 on easy sections; Task 2 250 words with 4 clear paragraphs

### Phase C · Year 2 · Exam skills (~6.0 → 6.5)

- Add Cambridge-style tasks (one section at a time)
- Listening Section 3; Reading matching headings
- Writing: Task 2 agree/disagree + advantages/disadvantages; start Task 1
- Speaking Part 3: why / compared with / in the long run
- **Exit:** 6.5-looking scripts after rewrite; fewer meaning-breaking grammar errors

### Phase D · Year 3 · Push (~6.5 → 7.0)

- Weekly mini-mock (not a full 2.5-hour test every week)
- Writing: hedging, precise verbs, less translation
- Listening Section 4 notes; Reading True/False/Not Given discipline
- Speaking: flex answers, not memorised speeches
- **Exit:** 7.0-looking on 2 skills; writing still the usual bottleneck

### Phase E · Years 4–5 · Precision (~7.0 → 7.5)

- Full mock every 6–8 weeks only (protect energy)
- Range: paraphrase, collocation, pronunciation of high-frequency chunks
- Rewrite old band-6 essays until they sound like 7.5
- **Exit:** stable 7.0+ on practice, writing 7.0 with occasional 7.5 paragraphs

If life interrupts for a month: restart at **Phase A weekday blocks**, not at a new textbook.

## Materials (small set)

Use few sources. Add one only when the old one is finished.

| Skill | Phase A–B | Phase C–E |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | BBC 6 Minute English; graded talks | Cambridge Listening sections |
| Reading | news paragraph (BBC/Guardian short) | Cambridge Reading passages |
| Speaking | self-record Part 1 topics | Part 2/3 + mock with timer |
| Writing | 80–150 word opinions | Task 1 + Task 2 timed |
| Vocab | phrases from your own texts | Academic collocations from errors |

Official Cambridge tests are for **measurement**, not daily entertainment.

---

## Weekly close-out (Sunday, 15 minutes)

1. Tick what actually happened (not what you hoped)
2. Move 3 leftover errors into next week’s Error-loop
3. Choose **one** focus for next week (example: articles / examples / numbers)
4. Do not add extra books as a reward for a good week

## This week (Cycle 1, Week 0 — diagnostic)

Goal: prove the blocks work. No full mock.

- [ ] **Mon / Wed:** Write 80–120 words on *part-time work while studying* (same topic twice is fine)
- [ ] **Tue / Thu:** Record one 60-second answer: *How do you spend a normal weekday?*
- [ ] **Daily commute:** One 6-minute English audio; shadow 3 sentences
- [ ] **Tue or Thu:** Read 1 short news paragraph; copy 5 phrases into `vocab.md`
- [ ] **Fri:** Rewrite Monday’s paragraph using only 3 fixes
- [ ] **Sat:** 1 Listening Section 1 (or 10 minutes of a dialogue) + note 5 missed words
- [ ] **Sun:** Fill one row in `errors.md`; pick next week’s single focus
