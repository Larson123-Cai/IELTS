目的：让我看清你现在怎么读、怎么定位、怎么判断 True / False / Not Given。不要查中文译文，不要用翻译软件整段生成。词典查单词可以。

交作业时请附上：实际用时、Academic 还是 GT、目标分（若有）。

文章在 [`week-00-passage.md`](./week-00-passage.md)。先读文章，再回到这里答题。先不要打开 `reading/coach/`。

## 作业 A · 限时阅读（必做）

**Time: 20 minutes.** 读完文章并完成 Questions 1–10。

### Questions 1–5

Do the following statements agree with the information in the passage?

Write:

- **TRUE** if the statement agrees with the information
- **FALSE** if the statement contradicts the information
- **NOT GIVEN** if there is no information on this

1. Several cities have opened new sports centres and presented them as a public-health success.
2. Building more gyms is enough to make inactive people take regular exercise.
3. The writer says transport and food policy have a greater effect on health than sports facilities do.
4. People who live far from a sports centre are always less healthy than people who live nearby.
5. The writer recommends combining better facilities with changes in daily routines.

### Questions 6–8

Complete the sentences below. Choose **NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER** from the passage for each answer.

6. New sports centres were mainly used by people who already ______.
7. Walking or cycling to work is described as a form of ______ activity.
8. The writer argues that facilities matter, but they cannot replace ______.

### Questions 9–10

Choose the correct heading for Paragraphs B and D from the list.

- i. Why poor diet cancels out exercise
- ii. Who actually uses new sports centres
- iii. A combined approach to public health
- iv. How sports facilities are designed
- v. Daily movement outside the gym

9. Paragraph B
10. Paragraph D

## 作业 C · 用英语跟私教说两句（必做）

**Time: 10 minutes. 80–120 words.**

用英语写给我：

- 这篇文章你觉得难在哪（词汇、长句、题型，还是时间）
- 你平时怎么练阅读（刷题、看新闻、几乎不读……）
- 你每周大概能拿出多少时间给 Reading

这段同时当水平样本。请写真实情况，不必写得很正式。不要为了好看去查范文。
